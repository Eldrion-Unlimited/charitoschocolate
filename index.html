Our Story

Charo is a Pastry Chef de Partie specializing in hand-painted, hand-crafted chocolate bonbons that are as visually striking as they are unforgettable to taste.

Her work blends classical pastry technique with bold, modern flavor pairings — resulting in chocolates designed to surprise the palate and elevate the moment they’re served.

From fine-dining dessert service to custom gift collections, Charo’s Chocolate is created for people who believe chocolate should feel intentional, luxurious, and deeply memorable.
