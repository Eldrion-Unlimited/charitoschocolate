Our Story

Charito is a Pastry Chef de Partie specializing in hand-painted, handcrafted chocolate bonbons that are as visually striking as they are unforgettable to taste.

Her work blends classical pastry technique with bold, modern flavor pairings — chocolate designed to elevate the moment it’s served.

From refined dining rooms to custom gift collections, Charito’s Chocolate is created for people who believe chocolate should feel intentional, luxurious, and deeply memorable.
